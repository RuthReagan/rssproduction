# Progressive Discipline: A Fair Process That Holds Up

_A progressive discipline policy protects your organization and treats employees fairly. Learn how to structure and document discipline that holds up under scrutiny._

When performance or conduct problems arise, how you respond matters as much as what you decide. A progressive discipline framework gives employees a fair chance to improve and gives your organization the documentation it needs if termination becomes necessary.

## A clear, consistent structure

Progressive discipline typically moves from verbal coaching to written warning to final warning to termination. The exact steps matter less than applying them consistently so that similar situations are handled the same way for everyone.

## Document at every stage

Each step should be documented with specifics: what happened, what was expected, and what will happen if it continues. This record is your protection if a termination is later challenged as unfair or discriminatory.

## Know when to skip steps

Serious misconduct like violence, theft, or safety violations may justify immediate action. Your policy should preserve the flexibility to respond to severity while still applying judgment consistently across the organization.

## Key Takeaway

Progressive discipline balances fairness to employees with protection for the organization. Apply it consistently, document thoroughly, and keep room for judgment when conduct is severe.
