# Workplace Violence Prevention: Building a Safer Organization

_Workplace violence prevention protects your people and limits liability. Learn how to build a program that identifies risks and responds effectively._

Workplace violence is a risk every organization would rather not think about, which is exactly why so many are unprepared. A thoughtful prevention program protects your people and demonstrates the reasonable care the law expects.

## Assess your risks honestly

Different workplaces carry different risks, from cash handling to public facing roles to late night operations. An honest assessment of where and how violence could occur is the foundation of any effective prevention plan.

## Create clear reporting and response

Employees need a safe, simple way to report threats and concerning behavior, and they need to trust that reports lead to action. Clear procedures and trained responders turn early warning signs into prevention instead of tragedy.

## Train and prepare

Train managers to recognize warning signs and employees to respond in an emergency. Preparation does not create fear, it creates confidence and can save lives when seconds matter.

## Key Takeaway

Workplace violence prevention is both a safety obligation and a legal one. Assess risks honestly, build clear reporting, and prepare your people to respond.
