An HR consultant who works nationally but has never navigated Oklahoma's particulars, or the sovereignty questions that come with advising a tribal enterprise, can give advice that is technically correct and practically wrong.

## What to look for when hiring an HR consultant

Ask about actual executive experience, not just years in HR but years leading HR departments and owning the hard decisions. Ask whether they have worked in your industry and jurisdiction. And ask how they measure success, a good consultant talks about turnover, compliance exposure, and manager capability, not vague promises.

Finally, look for someone who plans to make your team stronger rather than permanently dependent. The best HR consulting leaves your organization more capable than it found it.

### How is an HR consultant different from an employment attorney?

An employment attorney advises on legal questions and represents you in disputes. An HR consultant builds the systems, policies, and leadership that keep you out of disputes in the first place, and works alongside your attorney when legal issues arise. The two roles complement each other.
