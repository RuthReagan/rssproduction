# Exit Interviews: Turning Departures Into Actionable Insight

_Exit interviews reveal why people really leave. Learn how to run them well and turn honest feedback into changes that improve retention._

Every departure carries information you can use, if you ask the right questions and actually listen. Done well, exit interviews surface the patterns behind turnover and point you toward the fixes that keep your remaining team.

## Create safety for honesty

Departing employees will only be candid if they trust the conversation will not burn bridges. A neutral interviewer, a calm tone, and genuine curiosity produce far more useful feedback than a defensive or rushed conversation.

## Ask about patterns, not just events

Go beyond the single reason someone gives for leaving. Ask about management, workload, growth, and culture so you can spot the themes that show up again and again across multiple exits.

## Close the loop

Feedback only matters if it drives action. Track what you hear, look for recurring issues, and make visible changes. When your team sees that departures lead to real improvements, trust and retention both grow.

## Key Takeaway

Exit interviews are a free source of honest insight into your culture. Collect the feedback, look for patterns, and act on what you learn.
