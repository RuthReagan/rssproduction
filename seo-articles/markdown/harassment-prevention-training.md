# Harassment Prevention Training That Changes Behavior

_Effective harassment prevention training does more than check a box. Learn how to build training that actually shapes a respectful workplace culture._

Harassment prevention training is too often a compliance box to check, a video everyone clicks through and forgets. Real training reduces risk and shapes culture by helping people recognize, prevent, and report problems before they escalate.

## Beyond the legal minimum

Meeting the legal requirement is the floor, not the goal. Effective training uses realistic scenarios, addresses bystander responsibility, and helps employees understand not just the rules but the impact of behavior on their colleagues.

## Train managers differently

Managers carry extra responsibility. They need to know how to receive a complaint, what their reporting duties are, and how to avoid retaliation. Manager specific training closes the gap where many organizations get into legal trouble.

## Reinforce it year round

A single annual session fades quickly. Reinforce expectations through policy, leadership example, and prompt, fair responses to concerns. Culture is shaped far more by what leaders tolerate than by any training video.

## Key Takeaway

Good harassment prevention training shapes behavior, not just compliance records. Make it realistic, train managers specifically, and back it with consistent action.
