# HR Metrics That Matter: Measuring What Actually Drives Results

_Not all HR metrics are worth tracking. Learn which people metrics reveal real problems and opportunities so you can make better workforce decisions._

You cannot improve what you do not measure, but tracking the wrong numbers wastes time and hides the real story. The right HR metrics turn gut feelings about your workforce into decisions you can defend and act on.

## Retention and turnover

Turnover rate, especially voluntary turnover among your best performers, is one of the clearest signals of organizational health. Break it down by department and tenure to find where and why you are losing people.

## Time to hire and cost of vacancy

How long roles sit open and what that vacancy costs in lost productivity tell you whether your recruiting process is working. These numbers often justify investment in better hiring long before leadership feels the pain.

## Engagement and internal mobility

Engagement scores and the rate at which people grow into new roles internally reveal whether your culture retains and develops talent. Rising internal promotion is one of the healthiest signs an organization can show.

## Key Takeaway

Track the handful of metrics that connect people decisions to business results. Turnover, time to hire, and engagement tell you more than a dashboard full of vanity numbers.
